Stitches Island Hospital – Privacy Policy

Developer: Ocean Glide Ltd · App: Stitches Island Hospital (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.stitchesislandhospital) · Effective: 9 November 2026

The short version

Stitches Island Hospital does not collect, store or share any personal information. Feedback you choose to send from the game is described below (see “Feedback you choose to send”). It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it works completely offline. Everything the game needs is saved on your device only.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your hospital, rooms, staff, money, research and mission progress in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information we collect

None, except the feedback you choose to send (see “Feedback you choose to send”). The app makes no network requests except to send feedback when you choose to (see “Feedback you choose to send”), does not use your device's advertising ID, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Feedback you choose to send

From time to time the game asks “How are we doing so far?”, and its main screen has a “Contact our developers directly” button. Both open a short form with a star rating and an optional comment. Only if you press Send, the game sends to Ocean Glide, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection: your star rating, your comment (if you wrote one), the name and version of the game, the language of the game and the platform (for example Android). It does not send your name, e-mail address, advertising ID or any other device identifier, and our server does not store your IP address. If there is no connection when you press Send, the feedback waits on your device and is sent when the connection returns.

The feedback is kept on our server (Google Cloud, European Union) and forwarded to our own e-mail inbox. We use it only to understand what players think and to improve our games. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, and we keep it for no longer than three years. Sending feedback is optional: nothing is sent unless you press Send. Please do not write personal information in your comment. Because feedback is not linked to you, we cannot tell which feedback is yours; to ask us to delete a comment, write to us with its approximate date and content and we will delete it.

Children

Stitches Island Hospital is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app contains no chat, social features, links to outside websites or third-party services. Parents and guardians can contact us at the address below with any question.

Purchases and advertising

Stitches Island Hospital contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds either, this page would name the ad network used and the data it collects, and would be updated before that version is released.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
