Plank Platoon – Privacy Policy

Developer: BJA-LTD (Jeff Auguste), Mauritius · App: Plank Platoon (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.loosescrews) · Effective: 15 October 2026 (advertising added in this version)

The short version

Plank Platoon has no accounts and no analytics, and the game itself does not collect, store or share any personal information: your progress is saved on your device only. The game shows ads through Google AdMob (a bottom banner, a full-screen ad after every few wins, and optional videos you can watch for coins). AdMob, run by Google, may use your device's advertising ID and similar device data to show and measure ads; in regions that require it you are asked for your choice first. Owners of the Master Mechanic Pass see no banner and no full-screen ads. Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your progress, coins, settings, achievements, personal best scores and a list of the items you bought in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app, and you can clear it at any time with the reset option in the game's settings.

Information we collect

The game itself collects nothing and has no accounts, analytics or crash reporting of its own. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Purchases go through the Google Play app. The one exception is advertising: the Google AdMob software built into the app collects and uses the data described under “Advertising” below.

Children

Plank Platoon is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no chat or social features; its only third-party service is Google AdMob (see “Advertising” below). Parents and guardians can contact us at the address below with any question.

Advertising

Plank Platoon uses Google AdMob to show ads: a banner at the bottom of the screen, a full-screen ad after every third win (never during a level, a purchase or an open menu, and never in the first 15 minutes of a session), and optional videos you choose to watch for coins, a diamond or a hint. Buying the Master Mechanic Pass removes the banner and the full-screen ads.

To show and measure ads, AdMob (Google) may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address (to estimate your general location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data and do not link it to your name or any account. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the game shows Google's consent form the first time you open it, before any ad is requested; you can review or change that choice later in the game's Settings (“Ad privacy choices”). On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google's own explanation of how it uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Plank Platoon offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, weapons, cosmetics and the Master Mechanic Pass). Every payment is processed by Google Play under its own terms and privacy policy: we never receive your name, email address or payment details. Google Play tells the game only which item was bought and gives it a purchase reference, so the game can deliver the item and restore one-time items if you reinstall. That reference stays on your device.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
