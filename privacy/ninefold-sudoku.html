Isle of Nines – Privacy Policy

Developer: BJA-LTD (Jeff Auguste), Mauritius · App: Isle of Nines, formerly Ninefold Sudoku (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.ninefoldsudoku) · Effective: 25 October 2026 (advertising, in-app purchases, the Screw Tight bonus game and the “Our games” page)

The short version

Isle of Nines has no accounts and no analytics, and the game itself does not collect, store or share any personal information: your progress is saved on your device only. The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional videos you choose to watch (for an extra hint or coins) and, after your first 10 wins, an occasional full-screen ad between levels. AdMob, run by Google, may use your device's advertising ID and similar device data to show and measure ads; in regions that require it you are asked for your choice first. Anyone who has bought anything in the game, or bought Remove Ads, sees no full-screen ads. Optional in-app purchases are handled entirely by Google Play.

The app also contains Screw Tight, an optional bonus puzzle game opened from the home screen, which follows exactly the same rules as the rest of the app (same coins, same ads, same purchases, nothing more collected).

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your current puzzle, progress, stars, settings, chosen language, coins, saved hints and a list of the items you bought (with Google Play's purchase reference) in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app. The "Reset game" button on the level map and "Reset all progress" in Settings clear your levels and stars; coins and purchases are kept.

Information we collect

The game itself collects nothing and has no accounts, analytics or crash reporting of its own. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files. Purchases go through the Google Play app. The “Our games” page lists our other games; if you choose to ask to become an early tester, it opens your own email app with a message for you to read and send yourself: the game sends nothing on its own and stores no address. The one exception is advertising: the Google AdMob software built into the app collects and uses the data described under “Advertising” below.

Children

Isle of Nines is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no chat or social features; its only third-party service is Google AdMob (see “Advertising” below). Parents and guardians can contact us at the address below with any question.

Advertising

Isle of Nines uses Google AdMob to show ads: optional videos you choose to watch (an extra hint when you are out of hints, double coins after a win, free coins in the shop, and boosts or double coins in the Screw Tight bonus game; at most 12 a day) and a full-screen ad when you leave a win screen (never before your 10th win, at most every second level and at least two minutes apart, never during a puzzle). There is no banner. Buying Remove Ads, or anything else in the shop, stops the full-screen ads; the optional videos stay available for their rewards.

To show and measure ads, AdMob (Google) may collect and use your device's advertising ID, your IP address (to estimate your general location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. We do not receive this data and do not link it to your name or any account. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some US states) the game shows Google's consent form after you accept the licence agreement, before any ad is requested; you can review or change that choice later in the game's Settings (“Ad privacy choices”). On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). Google's own explanation of how it uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Isle of Nines offers optional in-app purchases (coin packs, a hint pack, a Starter Pack, Remove Ads and the full Screw Tight bonus game; its first campaign is free). Every level can be played without paying. Every payment is processed by Google Play Billing under Google's own terms and privacy policy: we never receive your name, email address or payment details. Google Play tells the game only which item was bought and gives it a purchase reference, so the game can deliver the item once and restore one-time items (Remove Ads, Starter Pack, Screw Tight) if you reinstall ("Restore purchases" in the shop). That reference stays on your device.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
