Ninefold Sudoku – Privacy Policy

Developer: BJA-LTD (Jeff Auguste), Mauritius · App: Ninefold Sudoku (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.ninefoldsudoku) · Effective: 27 September 2026

The short version

Ninefold Sudoku does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it works completely offline. Everything the game needs is saved on your device only.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your current puzzle, difficulty, and progress in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information we collect

None. The app makes no network requests of any kind, does not use your device's advertising ID, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Ninefold Sudoku is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app contains no chat, social features, links to outside websites or third-party services. Parents and guardians can contact us at the address below with any question.

Purchases and advertising

Ninefold Sudoku contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. If a future version adds either, this page would name the ad network used and the data it collects, and would be updated before that version is released.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
