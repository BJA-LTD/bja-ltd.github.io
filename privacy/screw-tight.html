Screw Tight – Privacy Policy

Developer: Ocean Glide Ltd · App: Screw Tight (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.screwtight) · Effective: 9 November 2026

The short version

Screw Tight has no accounts and we do not collect your personal information. Your game progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob and sells optional in-game items through Google Play. Google, not us, processes the data those two services need, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, coins, boosters, purchases you made, screw skins, daily reward and settings in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows rewarded videos that you choose to watch (for example to double your coins or get a free hint). It also shows ads between levels, but only after the first 10 levels and never to players who bought anything. The ads come from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID, IP address and general (country or city level) location;
  • device and app information such as model, operating system, language and app version;
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad is requested. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy choices in the game's pause menu. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Google's use of this data is covered by how Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. We do not receive information that identifies you.

Purchases (Google Play)

Optional purchases (coins, Remove Ads, packs and screw skins) are paid through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation of which item was bought so it can deliver it. Google Play keeps a record of your purchases so you can restore one-time items.

Information we collect

None ourselves. The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or precise location. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases; the game itself works offline.

Feedback you choose to send

From time to time the game asks “How are we doing so far?”, and its main screen has a “Contact our developers directly” button. Both open a short form with a star rating and an optional comment. Only if you press Send, the game sends to Ocean Glide, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection: your star rating, your comment (if you wrote one), the name and version of the game, the language of the game and the platform (for example Android). It does not send your name, e-mail address, advertising ID or any other device identifier, and our server does not store your IP address. If there is no connection when you press Send, the feedback waits on your device and is sent when the connection returns.

The feedback is kept on our server (Google Cloud, European Union) and forwarded to our own e-mail inbox. We use it only to understand what players think and to improve our games. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, and we keep it for no longer than three years. Sending feedback is optional: nothing is sent unless you press Send. Please do not write personal information in your comment. Because feedback is not linked to you, we cannot tell which feedback is yours; to ask us to delete a comment, write to us with its approximate date and content and we will delete it.

Children

Screw Tight is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians can contact us at the address below with any question.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
