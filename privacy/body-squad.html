Body Squad – Privacy Policy

Developer: BJA-LTD (Jeff Auguste), Mauritius · App: Body Squad (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.bodysquad) · Effective: 29 September 2026

The short version

Body Squad does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it works completely offline. Everything the game needs is saved on your device only.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your progress, stars, Lab upgrades, kid-mode and sound settings in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information we collect

None. The app makes no network requests of any kind, does not use your device's advertising ID, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Age

Body Squad is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, and the app contains no chat, social features, links to outside websites or third-party services.

Purchases and advertising

The current version of Body Squad contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. A future version may add advertising. If it does, this page will be updated before that version is released to name the ad network used and the data it collects, and the app's store listing will be updated to match.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
