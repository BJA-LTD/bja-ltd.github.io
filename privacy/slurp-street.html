Slurp Street – Privacy Policy

Developer: BJA-LTD (Jeff Auguste), Mauritius · App: Slurp Street (Android) · Effective: 29 September 2026

The short version

Slurp Street does not collect, store or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it works completely offline. Everything the game needs is saved on your device only.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your cash, upgrades, shops, gems, achievements, tutorial progress and sound and haptics settings in the app's local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. The optional backup code in the game menu is generated on your device and is only shared if you choose to copy it somewhere yourself.

Information we collect

None. The app makes no network requests of any kind, does not use your device's advertising ID, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Slurp Street is suitable for players of all ages, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing about a child to collect, store, use or share. It has no accounts, chat, social features, user-generated content, advertising, in-app purchases, or links to outside websites or third-party services. Parents and guardians who have questions can contact us at the address below.

Purchases and advertising

This version of Slurp Street contains no advertising and no in-app purchases. The in-game cash and gems are game currency only and cannot be bought with real money. If we ever add advertising or purchases in a future version, this page will be updated before that version is released, and any advertising in a version available to children will use only ad networks that Google Play permits for child-directed apps.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
