Nut Stack – Privacy Policy

Developer: Ocean Glide Ltd · App: Nut Stack (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.nutstack) · Effective: 9 November 2026

The short version

Nut Stack has no accounts and does not collect, store or share any personal information. Feedback you choose to send from the game is described below (see “Feedback you choose to send”). It has no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking, and it plays completely offline. Everything the game needs is saved on your device only.

Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the game saves your progress, scores, unlocked items, achievements and settings in the app's local storage on your device. It also keeps, on the device only, the dates you accepted the licence agreement and a short record of any app errors, so a problem can be investigated if you choose to send it to us. This data never leaves your device unless you send it yourself, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Information we collect

None, except the feedback you choose to send (see “Feedback you choose to send”). The app does not use your device's advertising ID and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Writing to us

The “Contact our developers directly” button opens the feedback form described below (see “Feedback you choose to send”). The early-access tester sign-up opens your own email app with a message to us ready; nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself. We use what you write only to answer you and to improve our games.

Updates

When a newer version of the game is available, the game may ask whether you want to update. This check is made by Google Play on your device; the game itself sends nothing to us.

Purchases and advertising

Nut Stack contains no advertising and no in-app purchases in this version. If a future version adds either (for example optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, or purchases through Google Play Billing), this page will be updated before that version is released to name the service used and the data it processes.

Feedback you choose to send

From time to time the game asks “How are we doing so far?”, and its main screen has a “Contact our developers directly” button. Both open a short form with a star rating and an optional comment. Only if you press Send, the game sends to Ocean Glide, over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection: your star rating, your comment (if you wrote one), the name and version of the game, the language of the game and the platform (for example Android). It does not send your name, e-mail address, advertising ID or any other device identifier, and our server does not store your IP address. If there is no connection when you press Send, the feedback waits on your device and is sent when the connection returns.

The feedback is kept on our server (Google Cloud, European Union) and forwarded to our own e-mail inbox. We use it only to understand what players think and to improve our games. We do not sell it or share it with anyone, and we keep it for no longer than three years. Sending feedback is optional: nothing is sent unless you press Send. Please do not write personal information in your comment. Because feedback is not linked to you, we cannot tell which feedback is yours; to ask us to delete a comment, write to us with its approximate date and content and we will delete it.

Children

Nut Stack is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children, and the app contains no chat or social features. Parents and guardians can contact us at the address below with any question.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
