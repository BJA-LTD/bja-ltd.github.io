Kelthys World – Privacy Policy

Developer: Ocean Glide Ltd · App: Kelthys World (Android, package dev.jeffauguste.brickwild) · Effective: 7 November 2026

The short version

Kelthys World has no accounts and we do not collect your personal information. Your worlds, builds and progress stay on your device. This version shows no ads and sells nothing. It does not connect to any server of ours.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your worlds and builds, the courses you make in the Workshop, your Gems, your look (colours, caps, sprouts, backpacks), your companions and your settings in the app's local storage on your device. It also keeps, on the device only, the dates you accepted the licence agreement. This data never leaves your device unless you share it yourself, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Parents' corner

A parent can set a PIN, a daily play-time limit, the chat mode and whether online play is allowed. These settings stay on the device. The PIN is stored only as a scrambled (salted and hashed) value, never as the PIN itself.

Playing together

In this version, online play works only on your own local network, with a room server you run on your own computer. Nothing is sent to us. On that server the game shares, with the other players in the same world, the player name and look you pick, your position, the blocks you place or remove, and chat messages. Chat is filtered: links, e-mail addresses, phone numbers and rude words are blocked. If a player reports another player, the report and the last chat lines of that world are saved on that server for a person to review.

A later version will offer worlds over the internet on our own server. Before that version is released, this page will be updated with exactly what that server receives, how long it keeps it and how to ask us to delete it.

Companion players

The game includes computer-controlled companion players. They never ask for personal information and never ask anyone to buy anything.

Purchases and advertising

This version has no ads and no purchases: the Gems shop says "coming soon" and nothing can be bought. A later version will offer optional Gem packs through Google Play Billing (Google handles the payment and we never receive your card details) and optional rewarded videos from Google AdMob that you choose to watch. Before that version is released, this page will be updated with what Google Play Billing and Google AdMob process and how to change your choices.

Information we collect

None. The app does not access your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or location.

Children

Kelthys World is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents and guardians can use the Parents' corner described above, and can contact us at the address below with any question.

Google Play

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information such as installation statistics and crash reports under its own privacy policy. That data is not shared with us in a form that identifies you.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above.
